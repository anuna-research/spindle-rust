# Prove and verify a policy claim

1. Check policy support and resolve unsupported-feature diagnostics.
2. Compile the policy with its public private-input schema.
3. Supply private facts and select a conclusion.
4. Review the public disclosure summary and generate a proof.
5. Share the proof; select the expected policy and claim independently at verification.

Absent facts are not explicit negative facts. An undecided claim produces no proof of a negative tag.
Verification identifies facts as unattested. Failed commands produce errors without successful-looking output files.
For example, a public schema containing `(salary alice 120000)` discloses that candidate literal, but hides whether the holder selected it.
Identifiers inside a public claim remain visible. A claim also reveals whatever follows logically from its truth.
